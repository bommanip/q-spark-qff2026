    01 · THE CONCEPT

    What is superposition?

    A classical bit has a definite value of either 0 or 1. A qubit can exist in a quantum combination of these basis states. This is called superposition.

    Meet a qubit in superposition

    Q
    |0⟩
    +
    |1⟩

    A superposition is a quantum state with amplitudes for both |0⟩ and |1⟩.

    When we measure the qubit, we get either 0 or 1. The probabilities depend on the quantum state.

    For an equal superposition, each result has a 50% probability.

    Superposition is a genuine quantum state, not simply an ordinary bit secretly holding two values.

    03 · THE CONCEPT

    What is entanglement?

    Entanglement is a quantum phenomenon in which two or more qubits share a joint quantum state.

    Their measurement results can be strongly correlated, even when the qubits are far apart.

    Two connected qubits

    Q1
    ⟷
    Q2

    A shared quantum state can connect the measurement behaviour of both qubits.

    In a Bell state, measuring both qubits in the same basis produces correlated results.

    For the Bell state (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2, the possible measurement outcomes are 00 and 11.

    05 · QUICK COMPARISON

    Superposition vs Entanglement

    Superposition

    A single qubit can exist in a combination of basis states.

    Entanglement

    Multiple qubits share a joint quantum state that cannot be described as independent states of each qubit.
