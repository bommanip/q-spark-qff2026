🧠 Discover the Quantum Secret

🎲 Independent Dice

Each die uses its own three qubits. The outcomes are independent. Matching numbers can happen, but only by chance.

⚡ Entangled Dice

Three Bell pairs connect the corresponding qubits of the two dice. Their measurement outcomes are correlated, so the dice match.

Start with Independent Dice, then switch to Entangled Dice and compare the results.
