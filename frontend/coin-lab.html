🧠 Learn the Quantum Secret

A qubit can be in a superposition of the states |0⟩ and |1⟩ before measurement. In this game, we represent those outcomes as Heads and Tails.

The RY gate rotates the qubit. Changing the angle changes the probability of measuring each outcome. When we measure the qubit, we get one result: Heads or Tails.

Important: The coin is a visual metaphor, not a physical quantum coin. The quantum experiment is performed by Qiskit on a simulator.
