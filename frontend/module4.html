    01. What is a quantum algorithm?

    A quantum algorithm is a sequence of operations designed to use quantum properties such as superposition and entanglement to solve a computational problem.

    Quantum algorithms do not make every task faster. They can offer advantages for particular problems when the right algorithm and hardware are available.

    Classical computing vs Quantum computing

    Classical: Processes information using bits, represented by 0 or 1.

    Quantum: Uses qubits and quantum operations to process quantum states.

    02. Grover's Search Algorithm

    Imagine searching through a collection of possibilities to find one special answer.

    Grover's algorithm is a quantum search algorithm that can search an unstructured collection of N possibilities in roughly the square root of N queries, compared with a classical search that may require checking many possibilities.

    03. What is Shor's Algorithm?

    Shor's algorithm is a quantum algorithm for factoring large integers. Integer factorization means finding the smaller numbers that multiply together to produce a given number.

    A simple example

    15 = 3 × 5

    3 and 5 are factors of 15.

    Shor's algorithm is important because sufficiently powerful fault-tolerant quantum computers could use it to factor large numbers much more efficiently than known classical methods.

    Today's quantum computers are not yet capable of breaking widely used large-scale RSA encryption with Shor's algorithm.

    04. Quantum computing in the real world

    Healthcare and drug discovery

    Researchers are exploring quantum methods for modelling molecules and studying chemical systems.

    Materials and energy

    Quantum simulations may help researchers investigate materials, batteries and chemical processes.

    Cybersecurity

    Quantum computing has important implications for cryptography. Post-quantum cryptography develops methods designed to resist attacks from future quantum computers.

    Artificial intelligence

    Quantum machine learning is an active research area exploring how quantum circuits may be used in machine-learning workflows.
