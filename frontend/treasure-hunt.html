🧠 How Did You Find the Treasure?

01 · Superposition

Hadamard gates put both qubits into an equal superposition of four possible locations.

02 · Oracle

The oracle marks the chosen treasure state by changing its quantum phase.

03 · Diffusion

The diffusion operator amplifies the marked state's probability through quantum interference.

04 · Measurement

The simulator measures the qubits and returns the search results. In this ideal four-state example, one iteration finds the marked state.
