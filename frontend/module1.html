01 · THE BASICS

What is a Computer?

A computer is an electronic machine that receives information, processes it according to instructions, and produces a result.

How a Computer Works

↓ INPUT
⚙ PROCESS

Input → Processing → Output

For example, when you use a calculator, you enter numbers, the computer processes them, and displays the answer.

02 · CLASSICAL COMPUTING

Classical Computers and Bits

Traditional computers use tiny units of information called bits. Each bit can hold one of two possible values: 0 or 1.

Explore Classical Bits

0 BIT 1
1 BIT 2

A classical bit is either 0 or 1.

Bits are the basic building blocks of traditional computers. Photos, videos, messages and games are all represented using combinations of bits.

03 · THE QUANTUM WORLD

What is Quantum Computing?

Quantum computing is a new way of processing information using the principles of quantum physics.

Unlike classical computers, quantum computers use quantum bits, called qubits. These can behave in ways that ordinary bits cannot.

Classical Computer

Uses bits that are either 0 or 1.

Quantum Computer

Uses qubits that can experience superposition and entanglement.

04 · MEET THE QUBIT

What is a Qubit?

A qubit, or quantum bit, is the basic unit of information in a quantum computer.

A qubit can exist in a quantum superposition of the states |0⟩ and |1⟩. When measured, it produces a definite result of 0 or 1.

Meet Your First Qubit

Q
Quantum State

A qubit can be prepared in a superposition of |0⟩ and |1⟩.

Remember: Superposition does not mean that a qubit is simply a hidden ordinary bit. It is a genuine quantum state.
