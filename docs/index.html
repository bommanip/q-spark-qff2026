Quantum computing, as a board game.

Catch a floating coin, link two dice, and hunt for gold. Q-Spark is a beginner’s guide to quantum rules through moves you can predict and try.

Three lessons in the works

  • Coin Lab

    Float a coin, tilt it, and see how your move changes the odds of catching heads.

  • Linked Dice

    Roll independent dice and then try an entangled pair. Look for what changes when you compare the results.

  • Treasure Hunt

    Use Scanner and Nudge to boost the odds of finding gold on your first pick. Compare your result with a blind guess.

The planned game uses real Qiskit circuits on the local Aer simulator, with guided lessons in English and Kannada.
